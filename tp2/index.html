ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-2.06 - Watch TP2 (diagnostic)

This install writes separate parts and preserves Bluetooth pairings and settings - the NVS span is not touched. Do NOT tick "Erase device".

TP2 runs the real animated pet face and switches how each frame is presented. TAP cycles the mode (WIN / FULL / TEWIN), BOOT (short press) switches the link clock 40 / 20 MHz, PWR (short press) toggles the hostile bus load. The top strip shows the mode and the transport counters.

TP2 is a diagnostic, not a product build. Recovery is the product cable installer.
